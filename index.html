TikTok Auto Publisher

Automated video publishing tool for content creators

About

TikTok Auto Publisher is a desktop automation tool that helps content creators publish videos to TikTok via the official TikTok Content Posting API. The application runs locally on your machine and uses OAuth 2.0 for secure authentication.

Features: scheduled publishing, batch uploads, privacy controls, duet/stitch/comment management.

How It Works

The application authenticates via TikTok's OAuth 2.0 flow with PKCE. Once authorized, it can upload and publish videos directly through the TikTok API. All credentials are stored locally on your device.
